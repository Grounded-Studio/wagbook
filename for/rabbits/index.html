Wagbook for your rabbit

Rabbits show little when they’re ill. With Wagbook you keep a good eye on the vaccination, weight and food.

Vaccinations Wagbook suggests

  • Myxomatosis + RHD (1 and 2): every year

These are common schedules. Your vet decides what your pet needs; you adjust the schedule in the app.

What do you keep track of?

  • The yearly vaccination, with a reminder in advance
  • Weight: losing weight is often the first sign in rabbits
  • Pellets and hay, and when they run out
  • Vet visits, for example for the teeth
  • A diary: is he eating less, or fewer droppings?

Good to know

  • A rabbit that stops eating needs to see a vet the same day. Don’t wait.
  • Rabbit teeth keep growing. Plenty of hay helps wear them down.
  • Rabbits don’t cope well with heat. Keep the hutch in the shade in summer.

How Wagbook helps

Wagbook suggests the yearly vaccination for rabbits and reminds you in time. In the diary you write down what you notice, with a photo. Handy when the vet asks since when.
