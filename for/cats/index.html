Wagbook for your cat

Indoor or outdoor cat: your cat’s vaccinations, deworming, flea treatment, medication, weight and food in one place.

Vaccinations Wagbook suggests

  • Cat flu + panleukopenia: every year (or as your vet advises)
  • FeLV (feline leukaemia): every year, especially for outdoor cats
  • Rabies: every 3 years (required for travel)

These are common schedules. Your vet decides what your pet needs; you adjust the schedule in the app.

What do you keep track of?

  • Deworming, for example every 3 months
  • Flea treatment, indoor cats too
  • Weight, because cats hide being ill
  • Medication, for example a thyroid tablet
  • Food, and when the bag runs out

Good to know

  • Never use a dog flea product on your cat. Some products for dogs are toxic to cats.
  • A cat that loses weight may be ill. A weight chart shows it in time.
  • Lilies are very toxic to cats, even the pollen. First aid in the app lists what’s toxic.

How Wagbook helps

Wagbook suggests the common vaccinations for cats and reminds you about deworming and flea treatment. Medication comes with a reminder at the right time, with ‘Given’ and ‘Later’ buttons.
