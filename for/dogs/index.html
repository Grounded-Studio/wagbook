Wagbook for your dog

From puppy to senior: all your dog’s vaccinations, deworming, flea and tick treatments, medication, weight and food in one place.

Vaccinations Wagbook suggests

  • Core vaccine (DHP): every 3 years
  • Leptospirosis: every year
  • Kennel cough: every year
  • Rabies: every 3 years (required for travel)

These are common schedules. Your vet decides what your pet needs; you adjust the schedule in the app.

What do you keep track of?

  • Deworming, for example every 3 months
  • Flea and tick treatment, every month or every 3 months
  • Weight in kilos, with a chart
  • Vet visits with costs
  • Food, and when the bag runs out

Good to know

  • For a puppy, the vet gives the first vaccinations in a few rounds. Add them all, so you don’t forget the last one.
  • Taking your dog on holiday? The travel checklist checks whether microchip, passport and rabies shot are in order in time.
  • Big dogs age faster. Weigh your dog regularly and you’ll spot changes early.

How Wagbook helps

Wagbook suggests the common vaccinations for dogs, with the right repeat schedule. You get a reminder in advance and on the day itself. With the buttons on the profile you make an overview for the vet, the sitter, a trip or a missing poster.
