Wagbook for your bird

Birds hide being ill very well. A few grams less can already mean something. That’s why Wagbook weighs birds in grams.

Vaccinations Wagbook suggests

There are no standard vaccinations for birds. Your avian vet will tell you what’s needed.

What do you keep track of?

  • Weight in grams, with a chart
  • Vet visits, for example at an avian vet
  • Medication, with a reminder on time
  • Food, and when it runs out
  • A diary: moulting, eating, droppings

Good to know

  • Weigh your bird at a fixed time, for example in the morning before eating. Then the weighings compare well.
  • Is your bird fluffed up and quiet? That can be a sign of illness. Call an avian vet.
  • Non-stick pans that overheat give off fumes that are toxic to birds.

How Wagbook helps

There are no standard vaccinations for birds, so Wagbook doesn’t suggest them. You weigh in grams and the chart shows right away if your bird is losing weight.
