When can you travel? Work out the 21-day rule

After a first rabies vaccination you have to wait 21 days before crossing a border within the EU with your dog, cat or ferret. Enter the date of the shot, or your puppy’s or kitten’s date of birth.

A rabies shot is allowed from 12 weeks. Then another 21 days of waiting: about 15 weeks in total.

The rules at a glance

  • A microchip first, then the rabies shot. The other way round, the shot doesn’t count.
  • A European pet passport, issued by your vet.
  • Wait 21 days after a first rabies shot. Not again for a booster given on time.
  • For Ireland, Finland, Malta or Norway, your dog also needs a tapeworm treatment 1 to 5 days before arrival.
  • Outside the EU and for the United Kingdom, other rules apply. Always check the government website of your destination.
