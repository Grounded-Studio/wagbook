Food calculator: how long does a bag of food last?

Enter the bag size and how much your pet eats per day. You’ll see right away how long it lasts, when to buy new food and what it costs per day.

Tip: a measuring cup makes it easy to stick to the portion. Check the packaging for how much your pet should eat per day, and ask your vet if your pet is too heavy or too light.

This is a tool, not veterinary advice. In doubt? Ask your vet.
