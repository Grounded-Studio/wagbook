How old is your dog or cat in human years?

The old rule ‘one dog year is seven human years’ isn’t right. A dog is an adult after one year, and big dogs age faster than small ones. This calculator uses the common estimate that many vets use too.

Pet
Size

How we calculate

The first year counts as 15 human years, the second as 9. After that, every year counts as about 4 human years for cats and small dogs, 4.5 for medium dogs and 5.25 for large dogs. It stays an estimate: every animal ages differently.

This is a tool, not veterinary advice. In doubt? Ask your vet.
